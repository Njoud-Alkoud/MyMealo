## About MyMealo

**MyMealo** is an iOS and watchOS application designed to help users build more consistent meal habits in a simple and encouraging way.

The app supports users through personalized meal tracking, reminders, streaks, and progress insights. It also extends the experience to Apple Watch, making meal reminders accessible throughout the day.

## My Role

**iOS Developer**

I contributed to the development of MyMealo as part of a collaborative team during the Apple Developer Academy.

My work focused on implementing app functionality, supporting the iPhone and Apple Watch experience, testing features, and helping turn the product concept into a functional application.

## Key Features

* Personalized meal tracking
* Daily meal reminders
* Custom meal plan setup
* Streak tracking
* Progress insights
* Apple Watch reminders
* Simple and encouraging user experience
